HOW NSE INSIGHT THINKS

A transparent model, not a black box.

Market data tells us what the share price is doing. Company fundamentals tell us what the business is doing. NSE Insight combines the two, but it reduces confidence when verified evidence is missing.

Official market data→Verified company results→Derived ratios→Factor scores→Plain-English model view

FUNDAMENTAL PIPELINE

Verified report → ratios → score

The verified input stores facts such as EPS, book value, profit, revenue, equity, EBIT, finance cost and dividends. The build process derives P/E, P/B, ROE, margin, interest cover, dividend yield, payout ratio and growth using the latest saved NSE price where appropriate.

GUARDRAILS

Missing evidence reduces confidence

A security can have a market price without having enough verified financial evidence for a dependable model view. Governance flags can also override an otherwise strong quantitative score.

01Evidence before score

No source, no false confidence.

02Business before momentum

A rising price cannot substitute for weak fundamentals.

03Comparison before action

A score becomes more useful when you compare alternatives.

04Reassess when facts change

New results and announcements can change the thesis.
